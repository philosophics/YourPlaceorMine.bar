The Dry-Hire Model

How Mobile Event Bartending Works

By operating under a dry-hire framework, you skip standard 300% caterer markups. You buy the alcohol at retail; we provide everything else required for exceptional service.

01

Custom Menu Design

We work with you to engineer a balanced signature cocktail lineup reflecting your taste and theme, including house-made syrups, fresh garnishes, and craft bitters.

02

Calculated Shopping List

We provide an itemized retail shopping list specifying exact quantities of spirits, beer, wine, and champagne based on guest count and service duration.

03

Full-Service Execution

We supply the mobile bar station, bar tools, fresh mixers, juices, coolers, ice management, garnish station, and certified bartenders from load-in to breakdown.
